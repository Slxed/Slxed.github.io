# Visual directions

The applied design is **plate**: IBM Plex Serif for headings, IBM Plex Sans for body, IBM Plex Mono for small labels. Teal `#0F5C56` is the primary accent. Muted coral `#8E3D3A` in light mode and `#F0A8A0` in dark mode is the second accent. Coral marks the Currently line, timeline dots, project-row edges, link underlines, and coursework labels. Teal marks section rules and headings. Skill lines sit on a flat coral tint with a teal edge.

Coral `#8E3D3A` on the paper `#f6f3ec` is about 6.6:1. `#F0A8A0` on the dark background `#141816` is about 9.2:1. Teal `#0F5C56` on the paper is about 7.1:1. Dark-mode teal `#8fd9d2` on `#141816` is about 11:1. `#0F5C56` itself is too dim for text on the dark background, so dark mode uses the lighter teal.

Preview another direction with `?design=field` or `?design=survey`. To make one permanent, move its font and `--color-accent-2` lines into `:root` and into `[data-theme="dark"]`.

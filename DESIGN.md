# Visual directions

The applied design is **survey**: Source Serif 4 for headings, IBM Plex Sans for body, IBM Plex Mono for small labels. Teal `#0F5C56` stays the primary accent. The second accent is burnt orange `#9A4318` in light mode and `#F0A06A` in dark mode.

White text on `#0F5C56` is about 7.1:1 on the paper background. The burnt orange on the paper is about 5.9:1. In dark mode the light teal `#8fd9d2` on `#141816` is about 11:1, and `#F0A06A` on that background is about 8.5:1.

To preview another direction, open the site with `?design=field` or `?design=plate`. To make one permanent, move its font and `--color-accent-2` lines into `:root` and into `[data-theme="dark"]`.

## Field notes

Fraunces and Source Sans 3. Ochre `#7A4E0D` in light mode, `#E2B15A` in dark mode. Softer display serif. Ochre on the paper is about 6.5:1. `#E2B15A` on the dark background is about 9.1:1.

## Plate

IBM Plex Serif and IBM Plex Sans. Muted coral `#8E3D3A` in light mode, `#F0A8A0` in dark mode. Coral on the paper is about 6.6:1. `#F0A8A0` on the dark background is about 9.2:1.

Dark mode always lightens the teal to `#8fd9d2` so links stay readable. `#0F5C56` on the dark background is too dim for text.
